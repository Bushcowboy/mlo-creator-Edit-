Models that come with MLO Creator
=================================

These three .glb files are listed in the Window / door models tool of every project ("built in"). They are other people's work, used under their licences.
When you place one in an interior, MLO Creator writes the credit and licence into the README of the exported resource.

    by makinwhat (https://sketchfab.com/makinwhat)
    Licence: CC BY-NC 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)  -  NON-COMMERCIAL use only, credit required
    Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-window-systems-ffa9e879cdd04c3ba49d894f2f2ef5d3

door_wooden_old-18mb.glb  -  "Door Wooden Old-18MB"
    by Mehdi Shahsavan (https://sketchfab.com/ahmagh2e)
    Licence: CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  -  credit required
    Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/door-wooden-old-18mb-d2709483c3e644ed8d3aaa4998b5a47c

door_frame.glb  -  "Door Frame"
    by CrIsTaL (https://sketchfab.com/ArterLayll)
    Licence: CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  -  credit required
    Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/door-frame-c91101537bef410f98c67895746ec407

Check each licence before you use a resource made with them on a server that earns money. A project with a .glb of the same name in its own files uses that one instead.
